Envitron — Prototype 2: Ridge 36-Hour 90/10 Percentage Evaluation
Sub-team: Jenjira Huaisai and Friso Terpstra
Status: Preliminary research results (legacy split)
--------------------------------------------------------
Research goal:
How can the Ridge 36-hour forecast error be interpreted relative to a building's actual consumption?
--------------------------------------------------------
1. What we tested:
- Used one Farm building, approximately 15 months of history (18 June 2025 – 21 September 2026).
- Excluded 59 negative consumption records from the selected building.
- Forecasted 36 hours (144 x 15-minute steps).
- Compared Persistence, Copy-Yesterday, Ridge, and Ridge + Weather.
- Focused ONLY on the 90/10 chronological split in this isolated experiment.
- Reported MAE, RMSE, NMAE, NRMSE, and MAPE; excluded near-zero actuals (<0.1 kW) from MAPE.
--------------------------------------------------------
2. What we found (reference results, Ridge + Weather, 90/10):
- Mean actual consumption: 12.88 kW.
- MAE: 2.87 kW; RMSE: 3.96 kW.
- NMAE: 22.28%; NRMSE: 30.72%; MAPE: 23.44%.
- These are error metrics, not a percentage accuracy score.
- The charts compare all four approaches under the same 90/10 split.
--------------------------------------------------------
3. What it means:
- Relative metrics put the error in context of the building's typical consumption.
- MAPE and NMAE have different denominators and must not be treated as interchangeable.
- This experiment is a focused reporting view; earlier multi-validation research remains in Ridge_36h_Experiment.
- Legacy split may have overlapping 36-hour training targets at the test boundary.
--------------------------------------------------------
4. What to test next:
- Purge training origins with target windows crossing the test start; rerun all metrics.
- Compare all team prototypes on consistent periods and metric definitions.
- Evaluate additional buildings, periods, future weather, and large errors.
